# Bits & Pieces interactive collage

## User request
Replace the four generic fragment blocks with a playful, editorial collage of varied placeholder artifacts that can later be swapped for real personal material.

## Required behavior
- Six honest placeholders across photo, handwritten note, sketch, journal, and screenshot formats.
- Layered, overlapping desktop composition with deliberate rotations and depth.
- Selection state that promotes one artifact, rearranges/dims the rest, and reveals a story panel.
- Close by selecting again, close control, or Escape.
- Keyboard activation and focus state.
- GSAP entrance and state choreography, with a stable reduced-motion mode.
- Compact mobile composition without global horizontal overflow.

## Invariants
- Do not alter hero, portrait, bookshelf, article/project routes, or closing section.
- Do not invent personal facts; all artifact content must identify itself as provisional and replaceable.
- Keep review output noindex/nofollow.
- Preserve shelf physics and accessibility fallbacks.

## Verification
Focused source contracts, complete test/check/build gates, desktop/tablet/mobile screenshots, real click/keyboard/Escape interaction checks, reduced-motion and JavaScript-disabled checks, and regression verification of the shelf.
